Key Takeaways

Chores
Fixed chore schedules don't always work.
People have different work/college schedules.

Mental Load
Remembering and noticing chores is also a mental burden. 

Expenses
One person often pays first and asks others to reimburse.
WhatsApp, eSewa, Khalti, etc. are used separately.
Users need easier expense tracking and settlement.

Groceries
People don't always know what has already been purchased.
Shared grocery information gets lost in chats.
A shared grocery list could reduce confusion.

Communication
Reminding roommates can feel awkward or confrontational.
Users don't want to shame or rank roommates.
Roomie should use neutral and polite reminders.

AI
Users are open to AI helping with chores.
But they want to understand and control AI decisions.
AI should suggest, not force.

Main Product Insight
Roomie should reduce the remembering, calculating, coordinating, and awkward reminding that comes with shared living.
Less mental load → Less friction → Fairer shared living
