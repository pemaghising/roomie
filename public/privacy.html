Roomie privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Roomie is a household chores app. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your name, and your email address or phone number (used to sign you in).
  • Which household you belong to, and the chores, schedules and completions in it.
  • Your effort balance, a private running tally used to keep chores fair.
  • Your push notification subscription, if you turn on reminders.

Who can see it

Your effort balance is visible only to you. Housemates see the shared chores and who did them, not each other's balance. There is no leaderboard.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone. It is held with our hosting and database providers (Supabase and Vercel) only to run the app. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email.

Deletion

Ask and we will delete your data. Email pema.ghising133@gmail.com.
