# Brand guidelines photography: 16 shot briefs

These replace every photo in the Figma page `06 · Brand · Guidelines`. Each brief stands alone: open a **new ChatGPT chat** for each one, attach the files it lists, and paste the whole box.

**Rules behind every brief**
- **Casting:** Nepali women in their twenties, flatmates in a modern Kathmandu flat. One gender per image.
- **Colour:** natural colours, with one lilac moment at most.
- **Light:** bright, cool daylight.
- **No text or logos** unless the brief attaches them.
- **Phone screens:** they face away from the camera unless the brief attaches the real screen. The app cards are added in Figma afterwards, never drawn by the image model.

Save each result as `C:\Leapfrog\Bootcamp\Img assets\CHAGPT\G<number> <name>.png` and send Claude the path.
