# Roomie logo: image-generation prompts

Use these if you want more variations from Midjourney, ChatGPT/DALL·E, Ideogram or Recraft. Recraft and Ideogram handle vector-style logos best. Once you pick one, redraw it cleanly in Figma; generated logos are rarely production-ready.

## Main prompt (app icon)

A minimal, modern app icon logo for "Roomie", a calm household-sharing app for roommates. A soft, rounded house silhouette in deep lavender purple (#5C3B9B) with a tall pill-shaped door cut out of it. From the roof peak grows a small sprout on one thin dark green stem (#065F46) with two mint-green leaves (#34D399 and #6EE7B7) of clearly different sizes, one bigger and one smaller, yet visually balanced. The idea is "balanced, not equal". Placed on a pale lavender (#F3E8FF) rounded-square iOS app icon background. Flat vector style, no gradients, no outlines, no text, generous padding, friendly and cozy, simple geometric shapes with soft rounded corners, reads clearly at 32px. Style of Headspace, Calm and Notion app icons. White background around the icon, centered, high resolution.

**Negative prompt / avoid:** 3D, glossy, realistic, shadows, gradients, clip-art, cartoon faces, multiple houses, busy details, thin hairlines, text, letters, watermark, photorealism.

## Wordmark prompt

Logo wordmark "roomie" in lowercase, set in a bold high-contrast serif like Playfair Display Bold, near-black ink (#111827). The dot of the letter i is replaced by a tiny two-leaf sprout: one bigger leaf and one smaller leaf in mint green (#34D399, #6EE7B7) on a short dark green stem. Tight, elegant letter spacing, flat vector, centered on a clean off-white background (#FBFAFF), no other elements.

## Variation ideas to try

- **Two roofs, one house:** two overlapping rounded house shapes in lavender and peach (#FFEDD5) that share one door. Two roommates, one home.
- **Pill scale:** a pill-shaped balance scale whose two pans hold a small leaf and a big leaf and still sit level.
- **Door-as-R:** a lowercase "r" built from a pill-shaped door and a sprout leaf as the arm of the r.

## Tips

- Generate 4–8 at a time and pick for silhouette. Squint: if it still reads as "house + sprout" as a blur, it works as an app icon.
- Test the final icon at 1024, 180, 48 and 29px.
- Keep the Roomie palette: lavender #F3E8FF / #5C3B9B, mint #34D399 / #065F46, ink #111827.
